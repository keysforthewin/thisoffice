(https://kaylousberg.com · https://kaylousberg.itch.io). Attribution is not required for
CC0, but very much deserved — thank you, Kay, for making assets this good free for
everyone. If you use them too, consider supporting the project.

## User-imported characters — Mixamo (not distributed)

The app can import characters you download yourself from [Adobe Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com)
(drag-and-drop in the character picker). These assets are governed by Adobe's terms:
free to use inside your own projects, but **not redistributable as assets** — so they
are never committed to this repository. Imported characters are converted locally in
your browser and stored only in the gitignored `data/characters/` directory on your
machine. You are responsible for your own use of Mixamo content under Adobe's license.
